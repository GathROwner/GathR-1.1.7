# Listing video sound follows explicit playback intent

Craig confirmed Production OTA 01a188f6 works, then requested sound whenever someone explicitly presses Play, Replay or Resume. This additive change starts from its exact source 4176896 and documentation descendant cd1728c. The candidate keeps automatic card/detail starts muted, gives explicit inline starts an unmuted source, and unmutes before explicit inline/fullscreen resume or replay. Pausing, exposing controls, seeking and changing Fit/Fill do not themselves enable sound. The sound button can re-mute playback. Expanding a currently playing clip preserves its current mute choice. Returning cards start their next automatic exposure muted.
