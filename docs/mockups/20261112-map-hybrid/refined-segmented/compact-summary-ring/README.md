# Compact summary ring

Craig accepted the two-ring direction and requested that the house/count ring sit at a size coherent with the event and special counts. This revision reduces only the summary indicator and its capsule, preserving the accepted photo ring and synchronized highlights.

[Prototype](prototype.html), [Now](now.png), [second venue](selected.png), [Today](today.png), [halo origin](origin.png), [expanded](expanded.png), [narrow](narrow.png), [checks](CHECKS.json).

The summary ring is 32 points instead of 44, with a thinner 1.8-point stroke. Its numeral is 12 points, matching event/special numerals, with an 11-point house icon. The capsule is 120 by 36 points instead of 132 by 52; all three groups are vertically centered. An invisible six-point extension preserves a 44-point ring-button hit area. The main photo remains 40 points inside its unchanged 56-point segmented ring. The lower totals remain fixed while both highlights and the upper outline follow the displayed venue.

Browser checks passed for dimensions, matching numeral size, hit extension, both synchronized rings, fixed totals, halo origins, Now/Today colors, rotation, fan expansion, count sums, zero counts, reduced motion and 320px layout. Now, selected, narrow and expanded screenshots were independently reviewed for spacing, hierarchy and clipping. No page errors.

Counts/check-ins are fictional fixtures and images use Craig's supplied screenshot. Browser-only mockup; no app source or OTA changed. Native busy-map collision and final navigation behavior remain unverified. Prior design rationale and limitations are retained in [two linked rings](../two-linked-rings/README.md).
